# Foco feature portraits

Native Blender renders of the approved black iPhone model, viewed straight on with a slim pastel margin on both sides. The bottom of the phone intentionally extends beyond the frame. The phones are scaled down slightly to reveal more of the lower app screen, with the same narrow pastel margin above.

- Modos: sage `#D6DFD0`.
- Rutinas: powder blue `#D5E2EC`.
- Mi tiempo: muted peach `#E9D6CC`.

All three share the same camera, phone pose and neutral product lighting. Screen textures use the supplied Modos.png, Rutinas.png and Mi Tiempo.png screenshots. The screens contain example data. The pastel backgrounds are native Blender world shaders, with no external background image or glass pane.

Current delivery: `*-v2.webp`, 1200 × 1600 and 600 × 800 WebP, served through responsive srcset. Editable packed scenes and the render script are retained in the Foco project under `.dream-loop/scenes/08_feature_*.blend` and `.dream-loop/scripts/render_feature_showcase.py`.
