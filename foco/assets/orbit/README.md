# Foco card orbit

`foco-card-orbit-v2.mp4` is an original Blender 5.2 render of the existing Foco card model and four-stroke mark. No third-party stock imagery or additional props.

- Five seconds, 1280 × 1280, 60 fps, silent H.264 with progressive download.
- One continuous orbital movement. The position, velocity and acceleration match at the loop seam, and the card never stops rotating.
- The JPEG poster uses the same scene at its closest approach.
- The headline remains accessible HTML: “Lo mejor vuelve a ser tuyo.”
- Deferred video loading, visibility-aware playback, reduced-motion/data-saving poster, and an explicit play/pause control.

The editable `.blend`, motion source and decoded-frame checks are retained in the Foco workspace under `output/card-orbit-2026-09-22/`.
